What it does

Your child types the homework problem they are stuck on, in their own words, and Stuck asks the next question, one at a time, until they get there themselves. It will not give the answer or any step of it to their own problem, including when told the work is already handed in, and it can explain the idea behind a method. Each reply is meant to be a single question. It can slip, which is why every session is there for you to read. It never scolds and never mentions cheating; it just keeps tutoring. You can read every session: the problem as typed, what they were stuck on, what got them unstuck, and how many times they asked outright for the answer. Kindergarten to grade 12, and as many children as you add, each a first name and a grade.
