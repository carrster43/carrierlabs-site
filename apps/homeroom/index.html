What it does

Pick your state and Homeroom lays out the year's requirements at once, each with the statute it comes from and the date it was last checked: Texas, Florida, North Carolina, California, Ohio, Georgia, Pennsylvania, New York, Michigan and Illinois. Log attendance by the day, with hours where your state counts them, one record per child per day, so a correction never counts twice. Keep a portfolio of work samples by subject and date, each with a photo or a PDF, and a reading log. Tick each requirement as you meet it, put the ones with a month into your own calendar, and download a transcript you issue from the courses you enter.
