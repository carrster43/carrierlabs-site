What it does

Nine surfaces, up to three layered at once, and six ready scenes. Night Arcs change the rain over time: Storm passing builds to a peak and eases off over 90 minutes, Wind down fades over an hour, Rolling showers comes and goes. Save your own rains, switch between them mid-session with a crossfade, and paste a rain's code into Discord for another table. A timer from 15 minutes to 2 hours fades it out. Rain Window puts a live public camera from somewhere it is raining now on your screen while your rain plays, with any camera on the list and no time limit.

One payment, never a subscription. The license key comes by email from Gumroad, and you paste it into any browser you use.
