What it does

Start an arrangement with your key, then send the other parent an invite link. They join free, with no key, no account and no email address, and they read and add to the record at no cost. Messages go into a permanent record that neither of you can edit or delete, and each carries a cryptographic hash of the one before it, so a removed or altered message shows. Expenses carry the share asked of the other parent; only the other parent can agree, the amount is fixed once they answer, and only agreed expenses count toward one netted figure of what is outstanding. A calendar says which parent has each child on each day, with a reason when the usual pattern changes. Export the whole record for any period as a text file to keep, print or hand over: every message in the period, never a selection, what it does and does not prove, and the data anyone can check at carrierpress.com/handoff/verify/. Export needs a key of your own.
