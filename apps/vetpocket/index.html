What it does

Keep each pet's records in one place: species and breed, anything ongoing, weights, and every vaccination, worming and flea treatment with when the next one is due. When something is wrong, describe it in your own words and Vet Pocket answers the 2am question: go now, be seen today, book an appointment, or watch closely, with the questions your vet will ask, the signs that would change the answer, and rough cost ranges for each path. Some things, such as a cat that cannot pass urine, a swollen belly with retching, seizures or a suspected poison, are always treated as go now, whatever else is said.

A subscription that runs in a browser, renewing until you cancel. The iPhone app is not out yet.
