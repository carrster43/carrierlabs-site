Your street's noticeboard for organising things and lending things. One organiser opens the street and puts a card through every door; each neighbour types the code and is on it, free. No address is asked for or kept.

$4.99 a month or $39.99 a year, for the whole streetOne membership covers the whole street, and every neighbour joins free. It renews until you cancel in your Gumroad library. If it ends, the street stays readable and new posts, door cards and the flyer pause until it is renewed.

What it does

Your key opens one street. Print a door card for every house: each has its own code, works once and lasts 120 days, and whoever types it is on the street, free, with no account, no email address and no password. Print this week's flyer for the neighbours who will never open an app: what is on, what is on the lending shelf and how to join, with no names on it. Neighbours post happenings, one-off or repeating, say they are coming to the next one, list things to lend and record loans, and you look after the reports. Enter your key once on this page in the browser you use. A Carrier Press Access key on the Apps or All Access tier opens a street too.

A membership for the whole street, paid by whoever opens it. Every neighbour joins free. The iPhone app is not out yet.
