What it does

One spoken conversation a day with someone patient. Choose Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese or Japanese at your level, pick a situation (a bakery that has run out of what you wanted, a landlord and a heater that has stopped working, a pharmacist when you do not know the word, and three more), and the other person speaks first. You answer out loud. Replies are a sentence or two, read aloud a little slower than normal. At most one correction a turn, and only when a listener would not have understood; the conversation carries on either way, with no praise, no streaks and no points. Every correction is kept on the Corrections tab, with the minutes you spoke.

A subscription, in a browser. The iPhone app is not out yet.
