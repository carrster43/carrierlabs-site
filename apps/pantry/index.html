What it does

What is for dinner tonight, from what is already in the house. Type one line, "chicken thighs fridge 3" or just "rice", or photograph a grocery receipt, choose a PDF, or paste a receipt or an online order email, and Pantry turns the till's abbreviations back into food and guesses how long each thing keeps. Everything sorts soonest first, with the undated things at the bottom rather than hidden. Ask what you can make and you get up to two dinners that use up what goes off first, with the time it takes and what else it assumes you have. Write what you cannot eat once in Account and every idea is told to keep it out, including as a hidden ingredient, and to say so when nothing can be made without it. The same receipt read twice adds nothing twice.
