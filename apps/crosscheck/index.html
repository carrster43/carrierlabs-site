What it does

One key drafts the letters for one case, up to three of them: a query to the billing office naming the exact figures, a request for a fully itemised statement, and a letter asking your insurer to look at the claim again. Every sentence Crosscheck writes is a figure from your documents, a question or a request, never a legal claim or a threat. Your own reason goes in exactly as you wrote it. Copy, print or download each letter and send it yourself: Crosscheck sends nothing. Another bill needs another key, and you can choose how many cases at checkout. A Carrier Press Access key on the Apps or All Access tier drafts the letters for any case.
