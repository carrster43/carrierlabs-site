What it does

Income, deductions, quarterly payments and 1099s for one self-employed person. Add a receipt or a 1099 as a photo or PDF and it fills in the entry. Enter the estimated tax you plan to pay this year and it splits it across the four federal due dates and tracks what you have paid. Rentals comes with every key: your units, the lease on each, each unit's year on its numbered Schedule E lines, a question rather than a guess where a cost might be an improvement instead of a repair, and Schedule E working papers for each unit. Rental entries stay out of your Schedule C totals. It never applies a tax rule to you, and it is not tax advice.
