What it does

Log symptoms, flares, sleep, food and period in a minute a day. Pattern analysis, up to 5 a day, looks across your entries and shows what tends to happen together, and the summary prints for your doctor. When you start, you choose what you are tracking. Perimenopause has 36 symptoms in five groups, your cycle pattern, and a script of what to say at the appointment beside the report. Doses, for a GLP-1 medicine, logs each dose and the day it stepped up, your weight and seven common side effects, and counts how often each came up in the weeks after each step up. Today sets the week before a change beside the week after it, for a dose step or any change you mark. Record puts your own paperwork in one place: add a photo, PDF or pasted text of a visit summary, lab report, discharge paper or medication list, and it lists what the document says, line by line, with the words each line came from. You check each line against your original and keep what is right. What you keep becomes one dated timeline and a one-page summary of medicines, allergies, conditions and recent results to hand a new doctor. Up to 30 documents a day. Flare does not diagnose, explain or treat anything, Record does not say what any result means, and it is not a medical device.
