What it does

Add the person you look after, then their medicines exactly as the box says, with the times and days each is taken and, if you like, a photo of each tablet so whoever gives it can see what it looks like. Today lists each dose as it falls due. Mark it taken or skipped, with the reason, and PillProof keeps the time and the name of whoever recorded it, such as 8:04am, Cal; a slip can be undone. Updates are typed as health, how they are, or day to day, and one that needs doing can be marked done with the name of who did it. Appointments say what, when, where and who is taking them. Look after more than one person from the same browser. For the rest of the family there is a summary to download or print: the medicines as typed, the last seven days as recorded, what still needs doing and what is coming up.
