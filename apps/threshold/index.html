What it does

Add a child and their age, pick what they have, and Threshold lists every control that applies, most important first: the birthday on the account before anything else, because most other settings are worked out from it. Each control has its steps, why it matters, the platform's own instructions and the date the steps were last checked against them. When a platform changes a control you already set up, it shows under Changes. For a child of 13 to 17, the Agreement follows once the setup is done, at no extra charge: rules you and your teenager suggest, check-ins either of you writes, and freedoms that loosen on a date you both can see. Nothing is in force until both of you say yes, either of you can drop a rule or end the agreement, and your teenager sees everything you see.

One payment per child. It does not renew. The iPhone app is not out yet.
