What it does

Paste the school emails and add the letters and PDFs. FirstDay pulls out every date a parent has to act on, every form and every supply request, and puts them in one list: slips to sign and send back, money owed, things to be at, each with the letter it came from. A slip is tracked as signed and then as sent back. Every date carries its own confidence, and one FirstDay was not sure it read correctly is not scheduled: it waits at the top of the list for you to confirm or dismiss. Anything it read but could not file is shown to you. One supply list covers every letter and every child, and the names you add tell it which child a letter is about.
