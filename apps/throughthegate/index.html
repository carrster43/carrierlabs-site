What it does

Paste the text of your resume and see the record a competent general resume parser builds from it: the fields it kept, the job titles it renamed with your own wording beside them, and every piece that did not survive, each with the plain reason it was lost. It flags what is worth knowing, such as a date with no month or a gap, and says what to change where there is something to change. Parse as many versions as you like and compare them. The Applications tab tracks where you applied, and marks one with no reply in a month.

Runs in a browser: a monthly subscription that renews until you cancel, or a 90-day pass paid once. The iPhone app is not out yet.
